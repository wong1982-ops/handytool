Wizard — How Much Input Before Generating?

More inputs = better results, but more friction. Where's the sweet spot?

A

Minimal (1 step)

Just a theme prompt + "Generate" button. Maybe a style dropdown (modern/vintage/minimal).

Like the current design but full-screen with better presentation. Fastest path to results.

B

Guided (2-3 steps)

Step 1: Theme prompt — "What's this mood board for?"

Step 2: Visual preferences — pick from mood thumbnails (cozy, bold, elegant, playful, etc.)

Step 3 (optional): Color preference — warm/cool/neutral or pick a starting color

Better results because AI has more context. Still quick (~15 seconds to fill out).

C

Template-First

Show a gallery of pre-made mood board templates (Wedding, Brand Identity, Interior Design, Fashion, etc.).

User picks a template, then customizes with their theme text. AI generates based on template + theme.

Lowest friction for users who don't know where to start. But limits creativity.

My recommendation: B (Guided, 2-3 steps) — The mood thumbnails in step 2 make it feel creative rather than form-filling, and the extra context produces noticeably better AI output. Step 3 (color) can be skippable.
