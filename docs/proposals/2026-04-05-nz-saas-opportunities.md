# NZ SaaS Opportunities — Research & Proposals

**Date:** 2026-04-05
**Status:** Proposed (not yet committed to build)

## Summary

7 NZ-specific SaaS tool opportunities identified through market research. Ranked by market size, build complexity, and competition level. All could be built as tools on toolk1t.com.

---

## 1. Tradie Tax & Expense Tracker

**Target:** Sole traders, tradies, cleaners, landscapers (353,000+ sole trader businesses in NZ)

**Why NZ-specific:** IRD requires 7-year receipt retention. NZ has unique GST rules (15%, with zero-rated and exempt categories), provisional tax, ACC levies, and NZ-specific deductible categories (vehicle logbooks, tool depreciation, home office). Late GST filing incurs $250 fine + 1% monthly penalty. A simple receipt-scanning, GST-calculating, deduction-categorizing tool for NZ sole traders would fill the gap between "doing nothing" and paying $50+/month for Xero.

- **Market size:** Large
- **Complexity:** Medium (receipt OCR, IRD GST period logic, NZ tax categories)
- **Competition:** Medium (Hnry, Xero, MYOB exist but expensive/complex; no free lightweight tool)
- **Revenue model:** Freemium — free basic tracking, paid for OCR/export

## 2. Healthy Homes Compliance Checker

**Target:** Landlords and property managers (~200,000+ rental property owners, ~600,000 renting households)

**Why NZ-specific:** As of 1 July 2025, ALL private rental properties must comply with Healthy Homes Standards. Landlords must provide a signed compliance statement with every new/renewed tenancy covering 5 standards: heating, insulation, ventilation, moisture/drainage, draught-stopping. No interactive tool exists that walks landlords through all standards, calculates heating requirements by room/climate zone, and generates the legally required compliance statement.

- **Market size:** Large (mandatory compliance)
- **Complexity:** Low-Medium (form-based calculator, PDF generation)
- **Competition:** Low (only government PDFs and basic checklists exist)
- **Revenue model:** Free tool with premium multi-property tracking

---

## 3. WorkSafe H&S Compliance Toolkit

**Target:** Small business owners / PCBUs (all 617,000 NZ enterprises)

**Why NZ-specific:** Under the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015, every PCBU must identify hazards, assess risks, implement controls, train workers, and keep records. Penalties up to $3M for corporates, $600K for individuals. Most small businesses lack dedicated H&S staff. No free interactive self-assessment tool exists.

- **Market size:** Large (legally obligated)
- **Complexity:** Medium (industry-specific templates, checklist logic, document generation)
- **Competition:** Low (SaferMe and paid platforms exist, nothing free for micro-businesses)
- **Revenue model:** Free basic toolkit, paid industry-specific add-ons

---

## 4. Immigration Visa Pathway Finder

**Target:** Prospective migrants, international students, employers (1M+ visa applications in 2025)

**Why NZ-specific:** NZ immigration system mid-overhaul. Points-based Skilled Migrant Category is confusing. Multiple pathways (work, student, resident, partner) with different requirements. Green List occupations frequently updated. No comprehensive free tool covers ALL visa pathways with eligibility checks and document checklists.

- **Market size:** Large (millions researching NZ immigration)
- **Complexity:** Low-Medium (rule-based logic, form wizard)
- **Competition:** Medium (basic calculators exist, none comprehensive and free)
- **Revenue model:** Free tool, monetize via SEO traffic + affiliate links to immigration advisers
- **SEO value:** Very high (high-intent searches, evergreen demand)

---

## 5. AML/CFT Compliance Tool

**Target:** Accountants, lawyers, real estate agents (15,000-20,000 reporting entities)

**Why NZ-specific:** NZ's AML/CFT regime expanded. DIA is sole regulator from June 2025. Small firms must maintain compliance programmes but existing software costs $200+/month. A lightweight tool for risk assessment, compliance programme generation, and basic CDD records would fill the gap.

- **Market size:** Medium
- **Complexity:** Medium-High (legal compliance, document generation)
- **Competition:** Low at affordable tier
- **Revenue model:** Freemium — free risk assessment, paid for full compliance programme

---

## 6. Tenancy Agreement & Bond Manager

**Target:** Self-managing landlords (tens of thousands with 1-2 properties)

**Why NZ-specific:** NZ tenancy agreements must follow Residential Tenancies Act format. 2024 Amendment Act introduced new rules: 90-day no-cause termination, pet bonds, anti-retaliation protections, meth contamination rules (2026). Self-managing landlords need compliant agreement generation, bond calculation/lodging, rent tracking, and correct notice forms.

- **Market size:** Medium-Large
- **Complexity:** Low (template generation, form logic)
- **Competition:** Low-Medium (myRent at $17/month is the main player)
- **Revenue model:** Free single-tenancy, paid for multi-property

---

## 7. E-commerce Readiness Calculator

**Target:** Small businesses not yet selling online (68% generate <10% revenue online)

**Why NZ-specific:** NZ small businesses falling behind APAC peers in digital adoption. $3.54B e-commerce market in 2026. A free assessment that evaluates readiness, recommends platforms, estimates NZD setup costs, covers NZ-specific requirements (GST on online sales, Consumer Guarantees Act), and provides a step-by-step launch plan.

- **Market size:** Large
- **Complexity:** Low (quiz/assessment format)
- **Competition:** Low (no NZ-focused tool exists)
- **Revenue model:** Free tool, affiliate revenue from platform recommendations
